Room-file character legend
===========================
Every character that appears in the assets/rooms/*.txt grids (outside the
@name/@levels header lines), grouped by role. Unicode box-drawing glyphs are
double-line ('═'/'║' family) with mixed single/double variants used only at
door caps, where the single-line stub marks the door opening.

Structural
----------
.   U+002E  FULL STOP                      floor (walkable)
(sp) U+0020 SPACE                          void (outside the room)

Straight walls / corners (no door)
-----------------------------------
═   U+2550  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE HORIZONTAL       horizontal wall
║   U+2551  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE VERTICAL         vertical wall
╔   U+2554  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE DOWN AND RIGHT   corner (opens south+east)
╗   U+2557  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE DOWN AND LEFT    corner (opens south+west)
╚   U+255A  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE UP AND RIGHT     corner (opens north+east)
╝   U+255D  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE UP AND LEFT      corner (opens north+west)

Wall junctions (3- or 4-way, no door)
--------------------------------------
╠   U+2560  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE VERTICAL AND RIGHT     T opening east
╣   U+2563  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE VERTICAL AND LEFT      T opening west
╦   U+2566  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE DOWN AND HORIZONTAL    T opening south
╩   U+2569  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE UP AND HORIZONTAL      T opening north
╬   U+256C  BOX DRAWINGS DOUBLE VERTICAL AND HORIZONTAL  4-way cross

Door caps (corner-shaped: wall continues one way, door is the other)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
╛   U+255B  BOX DRAWINGS UP SINGLE AND LEFT DOUBLE     door's left cap, top wall
╘   U+2558  BOX DRAWINGS UP SINGLE AND RIGHT DOUBLE    door's right cap, top wall
╕   U+2555  BOX DRAWINGS DOWN SINGLE AND LEFT DOUBLE   door's left cap, bottom wall
╒   U+2552  BOX DRAWINGS DOWN SINGLE AND RIGHT DOUBLE  door's right cap, bottom wall
╜   U+255C  BOX DRAWINGS UP DOUBLE AND LEFT SINGLE     door's top cap, left wall
╙   U+2559  BOX DRAWINGS UP DOUBLE AND RIGHT SINGLE    door's top cap, right wall
╖   U+2556  BOX DRAWINGS DOWN DOUBLE AND LEFT SINGLE   door's bottom cap, left wall
╓   U+2553  BOX DRAWINGS DOWN DOUBLE AND RIGHT SINGLE  door's bottom cap, right wall

Wall caps (T-junction-shaped: a wall junction cap)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
╥   U+2565  BOX DRAWINGS DOWN DOUBLE AND HORIZONTAL SINGLE  horizontal wall run
╨   U+2568  BOX DRAWINGS UP DOUBLE AND HORIZONTAL SINGLE    horizontal wall run

Doors
-----
1 2 3 4     door number/label, sits between its two caps (e.g. ╛1╘).
            By convention across these files: 1=north, 2=east, 3=south,
            4=west, though the number is just an id read by the room
            loader, not a compass direction.

Spawns (sit on floor tiles)
----------------------------
E   enemy spawn point
L   loot/item spawn point
o   pillar (blocks movement, floor-like otherwise)

Notes
-----
- The room loader (src/preload/room_loader.cpp) now recognizes the
  double-line box-drawing wall glyphs (straight / corner / junction) as
  Wall tiles, and the single/double door-cap glyphs (both the corner-shaped
  set and the T-junction-shaped ╥/╨) as DoorCap tiles. Each tile keeps its
  authored glyph; '#' is no longer used in room grids.
- Locked doors: the TileType::DoorLocked variant and its '⚿' (U+26BF)
  rendering exist, but nothing marks a door locked yet -- the planned
  trigger is a per-level flag in the level's map.json. '⚿' is never
  authored in the room .txt grids, so it does not appear among the grid
  characters above.
- scripts/convert-room-walls.py mechanically converts plain '#'-wall room
  files into the straight/corner/junction and corner-shaped door-cap glyphs
  above. It does not emit the T-junction-shaped door caps (╥/╨); those were
  added by hand where a door sits on a wall junction.
